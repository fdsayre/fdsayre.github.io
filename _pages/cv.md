## Professional profile

Academic librarian with 14 years of experience building and managing programs, services and spaces for students, faculty and community audiences; developing teams of library technicians and student staff; and leading academic governance and hiring. My current work connects experiential learning, student belonging, critical technology education, sustainability, community engagement and research-creation. Earlier work in health-science librarianship focused on evidence-based practice, research reproducibility, and open science.

## Research and scholarship

### Carrier Bags for Making the Chthulucene: More-Than-Human Information Practices for Academic Makerspaces, 2025–present

- A research-creation project that combines critical reading, reflective writing, interviews, material research and iterative making to investigate how academic makerspaces can cultivate ecological thinking, care, more-than-human relations and critical attention to technology and materials.
- Six-month research-creation sabbatical undertaken July–December 2025; the project continues beyond the leave period.
- Developed skills and methods in natural dyeing, cyanotype on fabric, canvas waxing, machine embroidery, appliqué, bag construction and large-scale textile work.
- Received Research Ethics Board approval for interviews and conducted five interviews between Dec. 9, 2025, and Jan. 23, 2026.
- Shifted from an initially survey-heavy design toward research-creation grounded in material practice, place, narrative and interviews.
- Developed Mapping Material Matters, a collaborative Makerspace activity that traces materials' relationships to people, places, plants, animals and technological systems; implementation is planned for the 2026–2027 academic year.

### Understanding Successful Student Learning Experiences in Academic Makerspaces, 2025–present

- Designed a qualitative interview study examining how successful makerspace learners describe motivation, information practices, learning environments, pedagogies, values, persistence and belonging.
- Developed the research questions, recruitment process, interview protocol and analysis plan.
- Received Research Ethics Board approval on Aug. 14, 2025, and began recruitment in September 2025.
