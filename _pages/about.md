hi there,

I'm an academic librarian at Thompson Rivers University, where I run the [TRU Library Makerspace](https://makerspace.trubox.ca/) and co-chair the Librarians' Department. I build programs and learning spaces that help people become confident lifelong learners who have agency over information and technology.

My work brings together active learning, peer learning, belonging, critical engagement with technology, and questions about sustainability and place.

This site is a place to share projects and think in public, including ideas I'm still working through. You can [read my posts](/posts/), find my professional history on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/franklin-sayre/), or [email me](mailto:fsayre@tru.ca).
